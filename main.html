Indian Flag

Design and DimensionsProportion

The ratio of the flag's width (height) to its length is 2:3.Three Bands: Saffron, white, and green stripes are equal in width and length.Ashoka Chakra: A navy-blue wheel containing 24 evenly spaced spokes, modeled after the Lion Capital of Ashoka at Sarnath.Adoption Date: Officially adopted by the Constituent Assembly on July 22, 1947, and first hoisted on August 15, 1947.Designer: Conceptualized and designed by Pingali Venkayya

Saffron Band (Top)

Courage, strength, and sacrifice.Intent: It represents the spirit of renunciation and selflessness.Message: It reminds the nation's leaders and citizens to dedicate themselves to their work without looking for personal gain.

White Band (Middle)

Peace, truth, and purity.Intent: It represents the path of light and truth that guides the nation's conduct.Message: It symbolizes a commitment to maintaining peace internally and keeping honest relations with the rest of the world

Green Band (Bottom)

Prosperity, faith, and fertility.Intent: It represents the land, agriculture, and life-giving nature.Message: It shows the country’s deep connection to the soil and its reliance on plant life for growth and survival.

Ashoka Chakra (Center Wheel)

The wheel of the law of righteousness (Dharma).Intent: It represents continuous motion, progress, and change.Message: Modeled after the Sarnath Lion Capital of Emperor Ashoka, its 24 spokes represent the 24 hours of the day, delivering the message that there is life in movement and death in stagnation
